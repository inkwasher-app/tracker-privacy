Tattoo Removal Tracker — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Tattoo Removal Tracker is published by Leon Chun Git Chong ("we", "the developer").

The short version

This app does not collect your data. Everything you enter or photograph in the app stays on your device. We have no servers that receive your app data in this version, we do not run analytics or advertising trackers in the app, and we never sell personal information.

What the app stores, and where

  • Client profiles, tattoo records, session notes, laser settings, and photos you add are stored only in the app's local storage on your device.
  • If you use the app's backup/export feature, the export file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to send or save it. We do not receive a copy.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored data from your device, except for any export files you saved elsewhere.

Optional Studio Sync (Google Drive)

  • Studio Sync is off unless you turn it on. If you enable it, the app syncs your records and photos to a folder in Google Drive — either a folder it creates in your own Google account, or a studio folder another account has shared with you.
  • That data goes between your device and Google Drive only. We do not receive, access, or store a copy of it on our own servers, and only the Google accounts the folder owner has shared the folder with can open it.
  • Signing in uses Google's sign-in flow so the app can read and write that one sync folder. You can stop syncing at any time in Settings, and deleting the Drive folder removes the synced copy.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Photos / Camera: so you can add treatment photos to a record. Photos are read and stored on your device only.
  • Calendar (optional, only if you enable calendar matching): the app reads calendar events on your device to suggest which client a photo belongs to. Calendar data is processed on your device and is not transmitted to us.

You can decline or revoke any of these permissions in your device settings; the related feature simply won't work without it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store your app data on our servers in this version.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs that receive your data.
  • We do not sell or share personal information with third parties.

Children

The app is a professional record-keeping tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled (for example, adding optional cloud sync), this policy will be updated and the change will be described in the app before any data is sent anywhere.
